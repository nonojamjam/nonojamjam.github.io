01 · Noise-Robust Object Detection via Heterogeneous Relational KD (capstone)

2025.11 – 2026.09 · team lead, sole ML/code

Keep a lightweight detector working under satellite sensor noise. DINOv2 (ViT) → YOLOv8s-seg (CNN) distance-wise relational distillation, plus 50:50 mixed-noise training; teacher and adapter are removed at inference, so the deployed model is unchanged (11.8M / 22MB).

Re-measured Sept 2026 — Kaggle T4, 10 epochs, σ=0.3, box mAP50, seeds 0 and 1:

  • Noisy gain +0.163 is stable across seeds (±0.0005). Of it, +0.163 is noise training and +0.005 is distillation — small, but positive in all four comparisons.
  • It costs clean accuracy: −0.053 on clean tiles, also seed-stable. That trade is the honest headline; the recovery ratio I used to quote moved from 12 to 15 on a seed change, so it is gone.
  • Found and corrected a baseline that had silently trained for 2 epochs instead of 10 — the reason the earlier ratios looked better than they were.
  • Built the pipeline solo: RKD loss, custom Ultralytics trainer (SPPF hook + adapter + mixed noise), 3 framework-level bug fixes.

02 · An LLM Wiki System for High-Consequence Domains (KAERI · IAA 2026)

2026 · KAERI internship · first author · oral

The model writes wiki nodes as it works and reads them back through ambient context injection — a hook picks relevant nodes before every generation, no manual retrieval. Built for space-operations and nuclear knowledge, where a wrong answer is expensive; set up on the institute’s closed network and handed over as an internal service.

  • Evaluation: answers from one frontier model, graded blind by two frontier models from different families, retrieval condition hidden from the graders, gold-span rubric. 94% raw agreement.
  • Ambient injection recovered the answer-bearing node more often than a hybrid-retrieval baseline, and the gap widened as the question’s wording drifted from the source text. Accuracy up, refusals down.
  • Two negative findings the paper reports on itself: graph-level interventions did not help, and injecting the correct node directly still left substantial hallucination — so independent verification is needed, not more retrieval.
  • Limitations stated: pipeline-level comparison (not component-attributable), single-turn benchmark, no human raters yet.

What it establishes: knowledge access is not the bottleneck. A model with the right fact in front of it still answers wrong often enough to matter.

03 · VLM Robot Commands Gated by Forward Kinematics (KAERI · KSIIS 2026)

2026 · KAERI internship · first author · negative result

A vision-language model proposes where the arm should go; forward kinematics decides whether that pose is reachable before anything moves. The pipeline worked as a filter. The interesting result was what happened when I tried to make the VLM better at the physics by telling it more.

  • Controlled ablations — DOF 1→2→5; hints none / chain-of-thought / joint angles / image feedback; VLM reading vs. generating — to isolate where spatial reasoning fails.
  • Giving the VLM joint angles made it worse: 165.7 mm → 276.9 mm position error, task success 0/5 either way. It cannot extract absolute angles or rigid-body rotation from text, so more text just gave it more to get wrong.
  • A verifier from the same model family shares the model’s blind spots — self-checking is not checking.
  • Ran the loop solo: experiment design, data collection, visualization, report. Project discontinued July 2026; this negative result is what it produced.

What it establishes: language does not carry the physical quantities the task needs. The check has to come from outside the model — kinematics here; sensing and execution feedback in general.

Earlier · eLoran Time-of-Arrival under Random Sample Loss (NAVIS Lab)

2025.12 – 2026.02 · undergraduate researcher · algorithm design, evaluation, report

Where the signal-processing side of this comes from. I designed two compensation schemes for a receiver that randomly drops samples — one time-domain, one frequency-domain — and ran the 1,000-run Monte-Carlo study that decided whether to keep them. Neither survived; plain accumulation won.

  • Frequency-domain scheme gained +6.78 dB SNR and paid for it with a permanent +1.497 µs TOA bias and 146–219% worse error variance — a cycle slip. Higher SNR was the wrong thing to optimise.
  • The lesson carried forward: reconstructing a signal you never received corrupts the quantity you actually need. Same shape as the VLM result above.
  • Implementation stays with the lab; parameter-level details in person.
