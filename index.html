01 · Noise-Robust Object Detection via Heterogeneous Relational KD (capstone)

2025.11 – 2026.09 · team lead, sole ML/code

Keep a lightweight detector working under satellite sensor noise. DINOv2 (ViT) → YOLOv8s-seg (CNN) distance-wise relational distillation, plus 50:50 mixed-noise training; teacher and adapter are removed at inference, so the deployed model is unchanged (11.8M / 22MB).

02 · An LLM Wiki System for High-Consequence Domains (KAERI · IAA 2026)

2026 · KAERI internship · first author · oral

The model writes wiki nodes during operation and reads them back through ambient context injection: a hook selects relevant nodes before each generation without an explicit query. Built for space-operations and nuclear knowledge, where incorrect answers carry high cost; deployed on the institute’s closed network and handed over as an internal service.

  • Evaluation: answers from one frontier model, graded blind by two frontier models from different families, retrieval condition hidden from the graders, gold-span rubric. 94% raw agreement.
  • Ambient injection recovered the answer-bearing node more often than a hybrid-retrieval baseline, with the margin increasing as question wording diverged from the source text. Accuracy increased and refusals decreased.
  • Two negative findings reported in the paper: graph-level interventions gave no improvement, and direct injection of the correct node still left substantial hallucination, indicating that independent verification is required.
  • Limitations stated: pipeline-level comparison (not component-attributable), single-turn benchmark, no human raters yet.

Finding: access to correct knowledge does not by itself remove hallucination.

03 · VLM Robot Commands Gated by Forward Kinematics (KAERI · KSIIS 2026)

2026 · KAERI internship · first author · negative result

A vision-language model proposes an end-effector target; forward kinematics determines reachability before execution. The pipeline functioned as a filter. The reported result concerns what happened when the VLM was given additional physical information in text.

  • Controlled ablations — DOF 1→2→5; hints none / chain-of-thought / joint angles / image feedback; VLM reading vs. generating — to isolate where spatial reasoning fails.
  • Supplying joint angles increased position error from 165.7 mm to 276.9 mm; task success was 0/5 in both conditions. The VLM does not extract absolute angles or rigid-body rotation from text.
  • A verifier from the same model family shares the model’s failure modes; self-verification is not independent verification.
  • Experiment design, data collection, visualization and report by the author. The project was discontinued in July 2026; the negative result is its output.

Finding: language does not carry the physical quantities the task requires. Verification must be external to the model — kinematics in this study; sensing and execution feedback in general.

Earlier · eLoran Time-of-Arrival under Random Sample Loss (NAVIS Lab)

2025.12 – 2026.02 · undergraduate researcher · algorithm design, evaluation, report

Origin of the signal-processing component. Two compensation schemes for a receiver with random sample loss — one time-domain, one frequency-domain — were designed and evaluated over 1,000 Monte-Carlo runs. Neither outperformed plain accumulation.

  • The frequency-domain scheme gained +6.78 dB SNR at the cost of a permanent +1.497 µs TOA bias and 146–219% higher error variance (cycle slip). SNR was the wrong objective.
  • Reconstructing samples that were never received corrupts the quantity of interest — the same structure as the VLM result above.
  • The implementation is laboratory property; parameter-level details are available on request.
