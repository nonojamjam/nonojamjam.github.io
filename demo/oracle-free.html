Where is my hand? joint reading vs tracker

The gate, the memory and the planner all compute the hand from the joint readings. The tracker measures it without them. A gap over 5 cm (black line) means the reading is wrong, and the robot should fix it before it acts.

What is real here 무엇이 실제인가

PhysicsMuJoCo 3.15 (WebAssembly). Boxes are lifted by finger friction; a wrong grasp or a missed box happens in the physics.
CameraA fixed camera above the table. Each pixel is a ray cast into the scene (so the arm can occlude), with depth noise, dropped pixels and colour noise. Boxes are segmented by colour saturation and height, the top face is fitted with a minimum-area rectangle, and the target is the box with a short side under 36 mm and a rectangular top. Object ids from the renderer only paint the pixel colour.
FingertipA range tracker: four anchors, 2 cm range noise, occasional non-line-of-sight bias, solved by Gauss–Newton. The median of five reads is compared with the fingertip computed from the joint readings.
Hand tiltAn IMU: gravity direction in the hand frame with 1° noise.
Joint faultThe shoulder reading is 0.15 rad off. The gate, the memory and the planner all use the readings, so they are fooled together.
CalibrationJoint offsets estimated online from the tracker and the IMU (Gauss–Newton with a ridge prior, last 20 observations).
Pre-grasp gateBefore closing the fingers: at least 10 observations and a disagreement under 5 cm. Otherwise the arm moves to a few nearby points to look again, re-estimates, and only then grasps.
PlannerA scripted stand-in that makes a bad move 30 % of the time; every step is checked by forward kinematics before it runs. The study page has the same loop with local LLMs (gemma3 4B → qwen2.5-coder 32B).
Ground truthUsed only to score the outcome (placed, standing) and for the "sim only" error readout. Nothing that chooses an action reads it.
