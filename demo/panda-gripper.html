Live demo · Panda gripper, real contact physics

Juhyeok Park · 2026.10 · MuJoCo physics running in your browser · scripted stand-in planner, not an LLM

Asking an LLM for every step is slow and costly, so the robot replays steps it has done before. Memory can be wrong, so every step is checked before it runs, and when the check itself can be fooled, it is backed by an independent sensor.

LLM에게 매 걸음 묻는 것은 느리고 비싸서, 해 본 걸음은 기억으로 처리합니다. 기억은 틀릴 수 있으니 걸음마다 실행 전에 검사하고, 검사마저 속을 때는 독립된 센서로 확인합니다. 아래 세 질문을 차례로 눌러 보세요. 설정 두 개를 같은 18번 방문으로 돌린 결과가 각 질문의 답으로 채워집니다.

What is real here 무엇이 실제인가

PartIn this demo
PhysicsMuJoCo 3.15 (official WebAssembly build): rigid bodies, joint limits, finger–box contact and friction. Boxes are lifted by finger friction, not attached to the hand, so a wrong grasp, a knocked box or an empty grasp happens in the physics.
RobotFranka Emika Panda with its hand, from DeepMind's MuJoCo Menagerie (Apache-2.0). Visual meshes simplified to load faster; collision geometry unchanged.
PlannerA stand-in for an LLM, asked one step at a time: it returns the next joint move (at most 6 cm toward the pre-grasp point), but with a fixed probability (30 % or 50 %) it gets two joints badly wrong. Each request counts as one planner call.
GateChecks every step before it runs: forward kinematics of the proposed move must bring the fingertip closer, stay within joint limits, avoid jerks and keep the hand pointing down. Up to three regenerations, otherwise the step is skipped. It uses the robot's own joint readings.
MemorySteps that made progress, keyed by the situation (direction and distance to the goal, bands of three joints), replayed without a planner call; a replayed step that fails is deleted. Also where the target was in each scene — stored when the robot believed it succeeded, so without a touch check a wrong grasp is remembered too.
ChecksTouch: the closed finger opening must match the target width (32 mm ± 6 mm). Camera: target position with 3 mm noise. Fingertip sensor: measured fingertip with 2 mm noise, independent of the joint readings. Misread: the shoulder reading is 0.15 rad off. Left standing: once the hand has left and the box has come to rest, its long axis must be within 45° of vertical (true pose read from the simulator).

Every setting runs the same visits with the same camera noise and planner mistakes, so the numbers differ only because of the setting. Results from the study with a real LLM planner are on the study page; the earlier kinematic demo shows the same seven scenarios without contact physics.
