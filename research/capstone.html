위성 센서 노이즈 속에서도 가벼운 검출기가 동작하게 하는 연구입니다. 배포 모델은 그대로 두고 학습만 바꿨습니다.

Keep a lightweight detector working under satellite sensor noise. YOLOv8s-seg (CNN) is trained with 50:50 clean/noisy batches and, in the final version, a centred spatial distillation loss from DINOv2 (ViT) patch tokens; teacher and adapter are removed at inference, so the deployed model is unchanged (11.8M parameters / 22 MB).

  • 0.018 → 0.381 box mAP50 under noise (σ = 0.3) from noise training alone
  • +0.020 / +0.010 more from centred spatial distillation (seeds 0 / 1); relational KD added nothing
  • −0.034 clean-tile cost of noise training; with spatial distillation −0.020

2026.10 correction: the September numbers first shown here used a public Kaggle copy of iSAID whose validation tiles were 88.9% identical to training tiles, and a label-parsing bug that inflated boxes. Every condition was re-run on the official iSAID split with fixed labels; all numbers below are from that re-run (graduation thesis v2.1).

What I tried

The target is a detector small enough for an on-board computer that still works when the image is noisy. The obvious route — distil a large, robust vision transformer into a small CNN — took five attempts.

What the comparison says

Noise training does most of the work (0.018 → 0.381 noisy mAP50). Relational KD adds nothing on top. Centred spatial KD adds a smaller but repeated gain (+0.020 / +0.010) and also raises clean mAP50. Robustness is not free: noise training costs 0.034 on clean tiles.

Superseded September 2 × 2 (kept for the record)

On the leaky Kaggle split with inflated boxes, the 2 × 2 (with / without distillation × with / without noise training) gave baseline 0.015 / 0.284, distillation only 0.018 / 0.297, noise training 0.178 / 0.225, both 0.182 / 0.231 (noisy / clean), i.e. "+0.163, 97% from noise training, distillation +0.005". The direction — noise training dominates, relational KD adds little — held on the official split; the absolute numbers did not.

Most of the robustness came from what the model trained on. The teacher helped only once it taught where things are, with the shared, position-independent component removed from both sides.

Spatial distillation in detail (graduation thesis, 2026.11)

Source: J. Park, B.S. graduation thesis, Chungbuk National University (in preparation, v2.1), Tables 3–4 and Fig. 3.

Relational KD relates images to each other. Spatial distillation instead asks whether the teacher can help by teaching where: I aligned DINOv2 patch tokens to student features position by position (cosine distance, teacher tokens with outlier norms masked) and checked each variant against a control in which the teacher's token positions are shuffled per image.

All conditions include noise training. Kaggle T4, 10 epochs, σ = 0.3, box mAP50, last-epoch weights, every model re-evaluated on the same noisy images.

Small objects: approximate AP50 for boxes under 32² px at 640 px input (not COCO small AP). Deployed student unchanged: 11.8M parameters; teacher and adapter removed at inference.

In a short diagnostic run, plain alignment pushed student features toward one position-independent direction. Subtracting each image's mean token from teacher and student before the cosine distance (centring) removes that shared component and aligns each position's deviation. It is the only variant that beat both noise training alone and its own shuffled control. Across evaluation noise levels, centred alignment led at σ = 0.1–0.3 on both seeds, and in all six seed × noise-sample combinations at σ = 0.3.

What this does not show yet

Centring and λ changed together; plain alignment at λ = 2.5 landed in between (0.391), so the two effects are not separated. The shuffled controls and that check have one seed each. The main comparison has two training seeds, and at σ = 0.4 the sign flipped on seed 1. The bootstrap resamples patches cut from 458 source images, so the interval is likely too narrow. Only Gaussian noise was tested.

Teaching the student where things are helped under noise once the shared, position-independent component was removed from both sides — and on seed 0 the gain disappeared when the teacher's positions were shuffled.

Implementation

Sole author of the ML code: RKD loss, custom Ultralytics trainer (SPPF hook, adapter, mixed noise), three framework-level bug fixes.
